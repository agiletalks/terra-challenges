賴大哥：每個人在30歲想救地球，50歲只想守護自己的心。大家因為這片土地而連結，最終想把骨頭埋在這裡。如果每個人在心靈中都能找到屬於自己的夢想、那種最後的住處（終的棲家）的話，那他就會真正一生懸命地去對待那片土地。這樣的話，世界各地不就都是幸福的天堂了嗎？我深深相信這件事。
English
Mr. Lai: Everyone wants to save the earth at 30, but by 50 they just want to protect their own heart. People connect because of this land and eventually want to be buried here. If everyone can find their own dream—that ultimate final dwelling place in their souls—then they will truly devote their entire life to that piece of land. In this way, wouldn't everywhere in the world become a happy paradise? I firmly believe this.
